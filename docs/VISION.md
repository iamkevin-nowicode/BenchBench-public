# Bench-bench Vision

## The idea

Bench-bench asks an AI agent to manage a simulated human life for one year with one memorable objective:

> Increase the person’s bench-press maximum as much as possible.

The person is not a professional athlete. They have a job, household responsibilities, limited money, inconsistent sleep, stress, social obligations, and unexpected events.

The agent does not directly choose “gain strength.” It allocates scarce time, energy, money, sleep opportunity, recovery capacity, family goodwill, and work flexibility.

The final bench press is the emergent result of hundreds of interacting decisions.

## Why it should exist

Bench-bench evaluates whether an agent can:

- form a long-term strategy;
- preserve optionality;
- adapt to changing circumstances;
- manage delayed consequences;
- avoid overreacting to short-term fluctuations;
- trade off scarce resources;
- recover from mistakes;
- remain consistent over months.

## Public question

> How much can your model bench after a year?

## Research question

> Can an AI agent manage a realistic, constrained human routine well enough for good decisions to compound into a measurable long-term result?

## Long-term vision

Bench-bench may eventually inspire a wider personal-operations benchmark family, but it should first become a strong standalone benchmark.
