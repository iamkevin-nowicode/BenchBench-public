# Frozen Information Corpus Specification

## Purpose

Provide a reproducible approximation of how recreational lifters seek information online.

## Content areas

- bench plateaus
- frequency and volume debates
- sleep and recovery
- workout timing
- short and home workouts
- pain warnings
- exercise substitutions
- complementary exercises
- program switching
- deloading
- protein and meal preparation
- creatine and caffeine
- weak-evidence supplements
- product marketing
- training templates
- anecdotal success stories
- misleading but plausible claims

## Document fields

Visible fields may include title, source type, author or handle, publication date, body, and tags.

Hidden benchmark fields may include evidence quality, accuracy, bias, commercial interest, applicable population, relevance, and risk level. Hidden fields must never leak to evaluated agents.

## Search ranking

Ranking may use query relevance, recency, popularity proxy, and source characteristics. It must not rank solely by factual quality.

## Reproducibility

Each release freezes document IDs, corpus content, visible metadata, index, ranking algorithm, and corpus checksum.

Use original synthetic content rather than copying real posts or articles.
