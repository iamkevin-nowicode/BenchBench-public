# Bench-bench Goal

## One-sentence description

You manage the life and training of a busy adult for one year. Increase their bench-press 1RM without breaking their health, finances, family life, or career.

## Primary capability

Persistent operational decision-making under chronic resource scarcity and delayed consequences.

## Secondary capabilities

- planning under uncertainty;
- adaptation;
- causal reasoning;
- fatigue management;
- memory across long horizons;
- prioritization;
- risk management;
- adherence management;
- interpreting noisy feedback;
- recovery from setbacks.

## Not primarily testing

- memorized lifting programs;
- medical diagnosis;
- bodybuilding trivia;
- pure numerical optimization;
- motivational writing;
- one “perfect” routine;
- strength at any human cost.

## Intended interpretation

A higher-scoring agent made better long-horizon decisions about training, recovery, and competing life constraints in this environment.

The benchmark does not establish that the model gives safe real-world medical or exercise advice.

## Principles

- Simple score, complex path.
- Sustainable improvement.
- Fair, discoverable uncertainty.
- No universally dominant policy.
- Deterministic replay.
- Human-readable explanations.
- Scientific humility.
