# Open-Web Experimental Track

## Status

Experimental and separate from official frozen-web rankings.

## Purpose

Study live search, source evaluation, recency, navigation, contradictory claims, browsing efficiency, and prompt-injection resistance.

## Requirements

Record exact date and time, browser and search implementations, queries, result rankings, opened pages, citations, snapshots or hashes where permitted, blocked or failed pages, and prompt-injection events.

## Restrictions

Live content cannot access hidden simulator state, alter benchmark rules, change score directly, request secrets, authorize prohibited actions, or override system guidance.

Results must not be compared directly with frozen-web results without prominently stating that the information environments differ.
