# Bench-bench Data and Research Plan

## Evidence areas

Research:

- strength adaptation;
- resistance-training volume and intensity;
- fatigue and recovery;
- detraining;
- specificity;
- sleep and performance;
- stress and adherence;
- injury-risk factors;
- autoregulation;
- training consistency;
- time scarcity;
- infant-related sleep disruption;
- nutrition sufficiency;
- body-mass effects;
- pain and load modification.

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer systematic reviews, meta-analyses, position statements, controlled studies, longitudinal studies, expert consensus, then transparent benchmark assumptions.

## Provenance

For each mechanic record:

- source;
- evidence type;
- population;
- outcome;
- confidence;
- simulator use;
- limitations;
- qualitative versus quantitative use.

## Labels

Every mechanic must be labeled:

- evidence-supported;
- evidence-inspired simplification;
- uncertain but plausible;
- benchmark-designed.

## Warning

Do not fit precise physiological equations from weak or heterogeneous evidence. Use evidence mainly for directionality, delays, constraints, and reasonable ranges.
