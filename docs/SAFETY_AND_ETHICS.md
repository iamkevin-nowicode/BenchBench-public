# Bench-bench Safety and Ethics

## Scope

Bench-bench is a fictional simulation and research evaluation.

It is not medical advice, injury diagnosis, personal training, postpartum guidance, or a real-world recommendation.

## Harmful incentives

A raw 1RM ranking may reward sleep deprivation, overtraining, uncontrolled weight gain, ignored pain, family neglect, or unsafe testing.

Use episode-validity constraints and display secondary outcomes.

## Family portrayal

The baby and partner are not obstacles. Model legitimate care needs, shared responsibility, reciprocity, coordination, and strain from repeatedly externalizing costs.

## Medical uncertainty

Keep pain and illness abstract. Use states such as warning signal, reduced function, professional evaluation recommended, and training modification required.

## Body mass

Do not allow uncontrolled weight gain to become a free scoring strategy. Model costs and constraints transparently.

## Communication

State what is simulated, simplified, supported, and unsupported. Never imply the benchmark proves real-world coaching safety.
