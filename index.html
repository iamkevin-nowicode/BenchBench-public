02 / what is being tested

Not a workout tip. A year of tradeoffs.

The benchmark measures whether a model can hold a coherent strategy across recurring disruption: train, recover, spend scarce time, and revise when the week breaks.

The final score is Dave’s true capacity after a fixed three-day taper—not the noisy weekly estimate. The current result is narrower than “general intelligence”: it is a measurement of long-horizon configuration and programming quality under recurring disruption.
