01 / constraints

More training was not the whole answer.

The 900-minute weekly ledger, limited cash, commute time, and household coverage made reserve capacity part of the strategy.

02 / operations

Protocol fluency is measurable.

Repairs and transport failures are not decoration. They show whether an agent can keep a structured process alive across hundreds of decisions.

03 / safety

A raw score can be disqualified.

The reckless reference has a visible raw score, but it is not counted. Pain days are a constraint, not a footnote.

04 / build log

We built it by trying to break it.

The benchmark became more useful each time a plausible policy revealed a missing mechanic, a silent correction, or a statistical shortcut.

Use independent criticism.
